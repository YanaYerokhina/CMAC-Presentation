Terms used in this deck.

LTV
Loan-to-value: the loan as a share of the property’s appraised value.
Personal guarantee, 100% pro-rata
Each owner personally guarantees their ownership share of the full loan.
DSCR
Debt service coverage ratio: cash flow ÷ debt service, tested before or after distributions.
3-2-1-0 prepayment
A penalty of 3%, 2% and 1% of the balance in years one to three, then none.
Make whole / swap unwind
A prepayment cost that compensates the bank for lost interest or ends a rate swap at market value.
PV01
The present value of 0.01% (one basis point) of rate over the life of the loan.
